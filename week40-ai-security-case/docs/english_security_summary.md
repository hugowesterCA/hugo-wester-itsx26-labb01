## English Security Summary

* **Threat:** Suspected prompt injection via an uploaded external document containing hidden instructions.
* **Affected assets:** Internal documents accessible to the AI assistant, and the trust in its output.
* **Main impact:** The assistant's output can no longer be trusted. Data exposure is possible but not confirmed. Staff will have to read and summarise documents manually until the matter is resolved, which will reduce the organisation's efficiency.
* **Top control:** Audit logging, so an explanation can be confirmed. Mapping of the assistant's access rights is also needed to determine the scope of the potential data exposure.
* **Key limitation:** The cause of the incident is not yet established, and the organisation's logging and permissions are not mapped.
